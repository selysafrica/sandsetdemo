# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Four roles work in StandSet, each inside a task. The concessionnaire's administrator steers a franchise network from the Console: registering licensees, setting royalties anchored on an amount T, tracking payments, habilitations and audits, and publishing versions of the Kit's core content. Licensees (consulting firms, training bodies, institutions in West Africa) run a branded instance to guide several client organisations through ISO 9001:2026. Their consultants and trainers work only on the files assigned to them, often from a laptop on a client site or a phone on a mobile connection. Self-service companies (a quality manager working alone) run a single transition file without an intermediary.

## Product Purpose

Industrialise the transition of organisations from ISO 9001:2015 to ISO 9001:2026 before September 2029. Replace two standalone HTML prototypes (Console and Kit) with one multi-tenant platform: gap analysis clause by clause with a coverage score, a six-phase transition plan, a document register, three training paths and a branded synthesis report. Success is a licensee who can run ten client files without a spreadsheet, and an administrator who knows the state of the network and its royalties in ten seconds.

## Positioning

A structured, reproducible method for the 2026 transition, sold as a white-label franchise: the same rigorous register-like tool, wearing each licensee's brand.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, reassuring, clear. The voice of a quality auditor who is on your side: precise vocabulary, calm confidence, no hype. French first, formal "vous".

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards with gradient hero metrics and identical card grids; flashy fintech neon; decorative glassmorphism. Nothing that makes a compliance tool feel like a toy.

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: a quality-management tool must itself look orderly, traceable and consistent.
- The register as metaphor: references, clauses and amounts are recorded, dated and aligned like entries in an official ledger.
- Always show where the deadline is: progress is measured against September 2029.
- The tenant's brand, StandSet's structure: licensees own colour and logo, the platform owns layout and rigour.
- Light on the network: fast on West African mobile connections, tolerant of outages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, colour never the only carrier of a score or status.
